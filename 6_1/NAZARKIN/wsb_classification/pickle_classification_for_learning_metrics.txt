WSB hand-labelled dataset - pickle_classification_for_learning.json
==============================================================

Total posts          : 8000
Per year             : 1000 (2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026)
Sampling             : uniform random, seed 20260928, unfiltered
Classes              : 4
  The fifth, catch-all class from the original task spec (UNCERTAIN) was removed
  on purpose. It appears in no post label and nowhere in the JSON - each of the
  8000 posts carries exactly one of the four classes above. Ambiguous posts were
  forced to the best of the four via the tie-break rules in LABEL_RUBRIC.md.

Reading the numbers:
  - NOISE_MEME dominates (~58%). That is the real WSB mix: most posts are
    memes, one-liners, questions about investing process, P&L screenshots and
    chatter. This dataset is NOT class-balanced, on purpose - it samples WSB.
  - ANALYSIS_DD is the smallest class (~9%) and is most common in 2019 and
    2025-2026; in 2019-2023 Arctic Shift returns an empty body for roughly 70%
    of posts, so those years can only be judged from the title.
  - FINANCIAL_CLAIM includes relayed news facts and concrete position numbers
    (e.g. '200 calls, expiry friday'), which is why it beats GENUINE_OPINION.

Fields per post: id, year, created_utc, author, score, num_comments, flair,
                title, body, label
